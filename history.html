History of Mobility

From Horse-Drawn Carriages to Electric Vehicles

Transportation has evolved dramatically over time. Major innovations have improved speed, safety, connectivity, and efficiency while shaping how people move around the world.

Key Milestones in Transportation History

1886

First Automobile

Karl Benz developed one of the first practical automobiles powered by an internal combustion engine.

1908

Ford Model T

Henry Ford introduced mass production, making automobiles affordable to millions.

1997

Hybrid Vehicles

Hybrid technology combined gasoline and electric power for greater efficiency.

2008

Electric Vehicle Growth

Modern lithium-ion batteries helped electric vehicles become practical and desirable.

2020

Autonomous Driving

Self-driving technology and artificial intelligence became major transportation research areas.
