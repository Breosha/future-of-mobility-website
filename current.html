Leaders Driving the Future of Mobility

Modern mobility is being shaped by visionary leaders who are advancing electric vehicles, artificial intelligence, autonomous driving systems, and sustainable transportation technologies.

Elon Musk

As CEO of Tesla, Elon Musk accelerated the adoption of electric vehicles around the world. His work has helped push the automotive industry toward sustainable transportation and advanced vehicle technology.

Mary Barra

As CEO of General Motors, Mary Barra has led major investments in electric vehicles and autonomous driving technology. Her leadership is helping shape the future of transportation through innovation and sustainability.foundation for modern transportation.

Jensen Huang

As CEO of NVIDIA, Jensen Huang has been instrumental in advancing artificial intelligence and autonomous vehicle systems. NVIDIA's technology powers many of the computing platforms used in self-driving cars today.
