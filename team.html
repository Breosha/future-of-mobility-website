Breosha Young

Hi! I'm Breosha Young, a Computer Information Systems scholar at Florida A&M University with interests in mobility systems, transportation technology, and sustainable innovation. As a participant in an eMobility fellowship, I have had the opportunity to learn how transportation has evolved from the first automobiles to today's electric and autonomous vehicles. Through this experience, I have explored the technologies, innovators, and trends that continue to transform the way people travel and connect. I created this website to showcase some of what I have learned about the history of mobility, the pioneers who shaped the automotive industry, and the current leaders driving innovation forward. As technology continues to advance rapidly, the future of mobility will play an increasingly important role in creating safer, smarter, and more sustainable transportation systems.
